Before You Write

QUICK ANSWERS

How quickly will I hear back?
Within one business day for all inquiries. Property submissions with an address typically receive a callback the same day if submitted during business hours.
Is there any cost or obligation to reach out?
None. Property assessments and initial conversations are free, with no obligation to sell and no pressure to accept an offer. If selling isn't the right move for you, we'll tell you that.
What information should I include about my property?
The address, property type (single-family, 2–4 unit, condo), approximate condition, occupancy status, and your ideal timeline. Photos are helpful but not required for a first conversation.
Do you work with attorneys and estate executors?
Regularly. We're comfortable working through probate timelines, estate coordination, and attorney-led closings, and we can accommodate extended closing schedules when families need time.
How do I request the investor packet?
Select "Private lending / capital" or "Joint venture partnership" in the form above, or visit the Investor Information page. We'll send current deal terms, track record, and available opportunities.

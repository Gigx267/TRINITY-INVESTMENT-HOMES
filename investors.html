How We Protect Capital

Real estate can produce strong returns, but only when the downside is respected. These are the structural guardrails we apply to every capital partner — not aspirational talking points.

  • First-Lien Position

    Lender capital is secured by a recorded first-lien mortgage on the acquired property — senior to all other debt.

  • Sponsor Capital in Every Deal

    Trinity invests its own capital alongside every partner. We don't ask anyone to take risk we aren't taking ourselves.

  • Conservative Underwriting

    Realistic ARV, full holding costs, contingency reserves, and a downside scenario. Deals that fail the pessimistic case don't get funded.

  • Documented Terms & Reporting

    Every structure is governed by written terms with monthly project updates and distribution reporting — no verbal side deals.

Questions

INVESTOR FAQ

Who is eligible to invest?
Offerings are generally limited to accredited investors as defined under Rule 501 of Regulation D, and may be conducted under Rule 506(b) or 506(c). We confirm accreditation status before sharing definitive offering documents. Eligibility criteria for any specific offering are set out in the offering documents.
How is my capital secured?
Private lending is secured by a first-lien mortgage on the acquired property, recorded at closing and title-insured. Joint ventures are governed by a written operating agreement that defines contributions, distributions, and exit. Trinity also invests its own capital in every project alongside partners.
What returns can I expect?
Returns vary by structure, project, and market conditions. Private lending rates and JV profit splits are set out in the offering documents for each project. We do not guarantee returns, and any projection shown is illustrative only — not a promise of performance.
What is the minimum investment?
Minimums vary by opportunity — typically $50,000 for private lending, $100,000 for joint ventures, and $250,000 for portfolio participation. Specific minimums are stated in the offering documents for each project.
How long is my capital typically committed?
Private lending terms commonly run 6–12 months with defined maturity dates, subject to extension terms in the note. Joint ventures typically span the acquisition, renovation, and sale or refinance cycle — generally 6–18 months depending on the project.
What reporting will I receive?
Partners receive monthly project updates including photo progress, budget-vs-actual tracking, and distribution reporting where applicable. JV partners receive periodic financial summaries consistent with the operating agreement.
What happens if a project underperforms?
Every structure includes defined remedies and priority of payments. For lending, the first-lien position provides a claim on the underlying property. For JVs, the operating agreement defines distribution priorities and decision rights. We communicate proactively if a project deviates from plan.
